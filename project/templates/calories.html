How Our Calorie Calculator Works

Our calorie calculator estimates how many calories your body needs each day based on factors such as your age, gender, height, weight, and activity level. It first estimates the energy your body uses for essential functions at rest, known as your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR). Your activity level is then taken into account to estimate your total daily calorie needs. Based on the result, the calculator can help you understand how many calories you may need to maintain your weight or create a calorie deficit for fat loss while supporting your training and nutrition goals.
